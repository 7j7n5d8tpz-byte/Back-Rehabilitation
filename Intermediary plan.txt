# Training & Back Injury Context

## Injury

- I have a herniated disc at **L5/S1** since august 2024. I have been trying to rehab from the injury ever since, having good periods and bad periods. I had a MR scan in september 2025, that shows that I had a huge disc bulge (L5/S1). I suspect that I have had 2 herniated disc injuries since then, one in March 2026 and one recently in june 2026. Both L5/S1. Clearly, my lower back is not stable, hence I need a proper rehabilitation program. 
- My primary goal is to build strength and muscle while protecting my lower back and reducing the risk of future flare-ups.
- I want a program that supports long-term spine health rather than maximizing lifting numbers.

## Current Status

- I am able to strength train and am not looking for a rehab-only program.
- I perform rehabilitation exercises regularly at home:
  - Dead Bugs
  - Bird Dogs
- These exercises generally should not be duplicated in my gym program unless there is a specific reason.

## Training Goals

- Train **3 days per week** with a duration of around 1 hour per session. 
- Prefer **full-body workouts** rather than body-part splits.
- Emphasize:
  - Functional strength
  - Movement quality
  - Balanced muscle development
- Build muscle while maintaining a healthy spine.
- 15 minutes at home each day for easy rehabilitation exercises

## Programming Preferences

- Prioritize compound exercises.
- Include rehabilitation

## Overall Goal

Design a balanced strength program that:

- Trains every major muscle group across the three days
- include exercises for the nerveous system so I can get back to being 100% me. 
- Builds functional strength and muscle.
- Supports long-term spine health.
- Includes progressive overload while maintaining excellent technique.
